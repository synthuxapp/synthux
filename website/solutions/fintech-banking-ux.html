What the Fintech Rule Pack Audits

Built for High-Trust Financial Transactions

Generic usability checklists miss financial nuances. synthux's Fintech Rule Pack inspects every stage of money movement, portfolio management, and authentication.

01 · Transparency & Finality

Fee Breakdown & Irreversible Action Guardrails

Verifies that network fees, FX spreads, taxes, and net recipient amounts are disclosed before the final step, and checks that irreversible transfers include clear review summaries.

02 · Error Prevention

IBAN, Card & Numeric Input Formatting

Audits numeric inputs for 4-digit visual grouping, automatic thousand separators, currency unit labels, and real-time checksum validation to prevent costly transposition mistakes.

03 · Security & Recovery

Session Reassurance & Non-Cryptic Error Recovery

Checks that 2FA/biometric prompts explain why verification is requested, and ensures declined payment errors provide human-readable causes without wiping form inputs.

Fintech UX Audit FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I activate the Fintech Rule Pack in synthux?

Open the synthux side panel in Chrome, go to the Scan tab, and select "Fintech & Banking UX" under the UX Rule Pack selector before clicking Analyze Page.

Can we audit multi-step KYC or wire transfer flows across several screens?

Yes. Open synthux Flow Builder to capture each step of your transfer or KYC funnel, connect the nodes on the visual canvas, and run a cross-page consistency and drop-off audit.
