Why High-Security Teams Choose synthux

Mission-Critical Usability Without Compromising Information Security

In defense, aerospace, intelligence, and banking operations, interface ambiguity causes operator error. synthux audits high-density consoles entirely within your security boundary.

01 · Air-Gapped Architecture

Security & Deployment FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does synthux transmit any DOM data or screenshots outside the local machine?

Never in Local AI mode. All DOM inspection, axe-core accessibility checks, and screenshot capture occur inside your browser and are sent strictly to your local Ollama endpoint (e.g. http://localhost:11434).

Can our InfoSec team audit the extension source code before deployment?

Yes. synthux is 100% open source under the MIT License, scanned continuously with CodeQL and Dependabot, and contains zero analytics or telemetry scripts.
